> Use case: stylized-concept. One project-bound transparent game relic icon for 凌空之翼 (Soaring Wings): reduces incoming attack damage while forcing attacks toward the healthiest enemy. Subject: a compact pair of battered ivory feather wings attached to a small old bronze clasp, with a single muted red eye-like garnet in the clasp. Clear heavy silhouette at 64 pixels. Original hand-painted dark whimsical roguelike object, visually compatible with Slay the Spire 2 relic art: chunky uneven brushwork, irregular near-black brown outlines, exaggerated simple volumes, bone white feathers with olive-grey shadows, tarnished old gold and a small desaturated red gem. Wings asymmetric and slightly worn, compact object, not realistic angel wings. Centered in a square occupying 75 percent of canvas. True transparent alpha background. No background scenery, ground shadow, lettering, numbers, text, labels, border, plaque, UI, watermark or logo. No glossy 3D rendering, no flat vector style. Only one asset.
